# Taxonomic framework

| Category | What we claim | What we **don't** claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ERV lineage** | A phylogenetically coherent ERV radiation/group | A single germline founder |
| **ERV sublineage** | A supported phylogenetic subdivision of that lineage | Diversification necessarily occurred after germline colonisation |
| **ERV-derived repeat** | A recognizable derivative associated with a lineage/sublineage | That it constitutes an independently supported phylogenetic radiation |
