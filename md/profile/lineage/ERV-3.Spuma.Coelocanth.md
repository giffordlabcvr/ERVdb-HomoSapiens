# ERV-3.Spuma.Coelocanth (CoeEFV)

**ERV-3.Spuma.Coelacanth (CoeEFV)** is a foamy virus-like endogenous retrovirus identified in the coelacanth (*Latimeria chalumnae*). It was the first endogenous foamy virus reported from a fish and substantially extended the known evolutionary range of the foamy virus lineage.

CoeEFV retains the characteristic genome organisation of a foamy virus, including *gag*, *pol* and *env* genes flanked by long terminal repeats (LTRs). Its discovery provided the first evidence that foamy virus-like retroviruses infected early-diverging vertebrates.
