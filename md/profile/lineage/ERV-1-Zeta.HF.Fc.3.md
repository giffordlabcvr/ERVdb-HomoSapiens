# ERV-1-Zeta.HF.Fc.3 (CfERV-Fc)

**ERV-1-Zeta.HF.Fc.3**, also known as *Canis familiaris endogenous retrovirus type Fc (CfERV-Fc)*, is the canid representative of the mammalian ERV-Fc lineage, which independently colonised the germ line of multiple distinct mammalians hosts during the Cenozoic Era. Orthologous and polymorphic insertions indicate that CfERV-Fc remained active in canid ancestors for at least 20 million years, with germline invasions continuing into the ancestors of modern wolves and dogs within the last few million years (PMID: 30845962).

Phylogenetic analyses indicate that the ancestral canid virus was itself recombinant, combining ERV-Fc-derived *gag* and *pol* genes with an *env* gene related to that found in the retrovirus lineage that gave rise to HERV-W. Many CfERV-Fc loci retain extensive viral coding sequences, although no replication-competent provirus has been identified.
