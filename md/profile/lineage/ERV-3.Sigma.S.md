# ERV-3.Sigma.S (HERV-S)

**ERV-3.Sigma.S** (HERV-S) is a primate ERV lineage belonging to Clade III, the major retroviral radiation that also contains spumaviruses and diverse spumavirus-related ERVs found across mammals and other vertebrates. The name HERV-S derives from the serine-tRNA primer-binding-site (PBS) specificity used in its original classification. This PBS designation is retained as part of the historical name but does not define the lineage phylogenetically. In the human genome, HERV-S is represented by defective insertions, several of which retain recognisable gag, pro, pol and env regions.
