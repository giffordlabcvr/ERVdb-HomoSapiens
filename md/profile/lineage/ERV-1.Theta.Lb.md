# ERV-1.Theta.Lb (HERV-Lb)

**ERV-1.Theta.Lb (HERV-Lb)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage found in primates. Phylogenetic analyses place HERV-Lb within the **Theta** lineage, a deeply divergent group of vertebrate endogenous retroviruses represented in multiple mammalian orders.

Analysis of paired long terminal repeats (LTRs) suggests that HERV-Lb entered the primate germ line during the Eocene or earlier. Human HERV-Lb loci are highly degraded and no replication-competent representatives are known.

Closely related Theta lineages have also been identified in perissodactyls and other mammals, indicating that this ancient retroviral group persisted across diverse mammalian hosts for tens of millions of years.
