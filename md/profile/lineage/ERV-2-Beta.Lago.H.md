# ERV-2-Beta.Lago.H

**ERV-2.Beta.Oryctolagus.H (RERV-H)** is an endogenous betaretrovirus lineage found in the European rabbit (*Oryctolagus cuniculus*). The lineage was first characterised after sequences initially reported as a novel human retrovirus, **Human Retrovirus 5 (HRV-5)**, were shown to originate from rabbit genomic DNA rather than from human infection (PMID: 12072509).

HRV-5 was originally proposed as a previously unrecognised human retrovirus associated with rheumatoid arthritis and systemic lupus erythematosus. Subsequent cloning and sequencing demonstrated that the complete proviral genome was integrated within the rabbit genome, and analysis of the flanking DNA confirmed its endogenous origin. Rabbit mitochondrial DNA was also detected in several of the human DNA preparations used in the original studies, providing a likely explanation for the initial reports.

RERV-H remains of historical interest as one of the best-known examples of an endogenous retrovirus being mistaken for a novel human pathogen. The episode highlighted the importance of rigorous contamination controls in PCR-based pathogen discovery and demonstrated how endogenous retroviral sequences can complicate the identification of new viruses.
