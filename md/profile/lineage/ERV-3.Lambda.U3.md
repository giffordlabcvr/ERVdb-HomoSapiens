# ERV-3.Lambda.U3 (HERV-U3)

**ERV-3.Lambda.U3 (HERV-U3)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the Lambda group. Unlike most members of the broader HERV-L radiation, HERV-U3 retains a portion of the envelope (*env*) gene, allowing it to be recognised through transmembrane (TM)-based screens for endogenous retroviral envelope proteins.
