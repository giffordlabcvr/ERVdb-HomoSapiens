# ERV-2-Lenti.Archaeo.Dermoptera (DELV)

**ERV-2-Lenti.Archaeo.Dermoptera** (DELV; originally reported as ELVgv) is an endogenous lentivirus lineage identified in colugos (order Dermoptera). Orthologous insertions shared by the two extant colugo genera, Galeopterus and Cynocephalus, indicate that the lineage entered the dermopteran germ line before their divergence, establishing a minimum age of approximately 15 million years.

Molecular dating suggests that individual DELV integrations accumulated between approximately 20 and 40 million years ago, making DELV one of the oldest known endogenous lentivirus lineages.

Phylogenetically, DELV groups with the endogenous springhare lentivirus SpeLV, forming a distinct lentiviral lineage that has been provisionally designated Archaeolentivirus.
