# ERV-1.Zeta.W9.P (HERV-P)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9.P (HERV-P)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the W9 radiation of primate gammaretroviruses. The lineage was originally defined by a primer binding site complementary to proline tRNA and was among the earliest human endogenous retrovirus groups recognised through primer-binding-site-based classification.

Comparative analyses indicate that HERV-P entered the primate germ line during the Eocene or earlier. Human HERV-P loci are represented by highly degraded proviruses and associated long terminal repeat (LTR) families, including **LTR9** and **MER61**, and no replication-competent representatives are known.

Phylogenetic analyses indicate that the historical **HUERSP3** lineage belongs within HERV-P, whereas the similarly named **HUERSP1** and **HUERSP2** elements appear to represent distinct endogenous retrovirus lineages despite their similar historical nomenclature.
