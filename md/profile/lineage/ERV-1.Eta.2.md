# ERV-1.Eta.2 (PERV-E)

**Porcine endogenous retrovirus E (PERV-E)** is a clade I endogenous gammaretrovirus lineage found in the pig genome. It was first described in 2001 following the recovery of a complete proviral sequence whose *gag*, *pol* and *env* genes were closely related to those of the human endogenous retrovirus HERV4-1, a member of the HERV-E group (PMID: 11457988).

PERV-E-related proviruses have been detected in all domestic pig and wild-boar genomes examined, with multiple copies present in individual animals. Unlike the better-characterised PERV-A, PERV-B and PERV-C groups, PERV-E has not been shown to produce an infectious virus or to infect human cells.
