# ERV-2.Beta.K.HML8 (HERV-K-HML8)

**ERV-2.Beta.K.HML8 (HERV-K-HML8)** is an ancient endogenous betaretrovirus lineage belonging to the **Kappa (K)** sublineage of betaretroviruses (genus *Betaretrovirus*). Comparative genomic analyses indicate that HML8 colonised the genomes of **catarrhine primates** (Old World monkeys and apes) during the Oligocene and Miocene, with most integrations occurring between approximately **43 and 17 million years ago**. The lineage comprises around **78 proviral loci** and more than **500 solo long terminal repeats (LTRs)** in the human genome.

Unlike most HML lineages, HML8 can be divided into three well-supported evolutionary subgroups associated with the **MER11A**, **MER11B**, and **MER11C** LTR families. These subgroups appear to represent successive waves of germline colonisation, with the MER11C lineage significantly younger than MER11A and MER11B (PMID: 36309315).
