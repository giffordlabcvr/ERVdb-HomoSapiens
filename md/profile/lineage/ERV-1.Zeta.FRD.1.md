# ERV-1.Zeta.FRD.1 (HERV-FRD)

**ERV-1.Zeta.FRD.1 (HERV-FRD)** is an ancient clade I endogenous retrovirus lineage best known as the source of **Syncytin-2** (*ERVFRD-1*), an envelope-derived protein required for normal placental development in humans and other catarrhine primates.

Orthologous **Syncytin-2** loci shared by catarrhine primates indicate that the HERV-FRD *env* gene was domesticated during the Eocene, more than 40 million years ago. Unlike most HERV-FRD loci, which have accumulated extensive mutations, the Syncytin-2 *env* gene has been maintained by purifying selection. It retains the membrane fusion activity of a retroviral envelope protein and is expressed in the syncytiotrophoblast, where it contributes to placental development.
