# ERV-2.Beta.Archaeo.6 (HERV-K-HML6)

**ERV-2.Beta.Archaeo.6 (HERV-K-HML-6)** is an ancient lineage within the primate HERV-K radiation. Human HML-6 loci are represented by numerous degraded proviruses and solo long terminal repeats (LTRs). No replication-competent representatives are known.

Although HML6 is thought to be among the most ancient betaretroviral lineages in the primate germline, several HML-6 loci remain transcriptionally active in human tissues. HML-6-derived transcripts have been detected in both healthy and diseased tissues.
