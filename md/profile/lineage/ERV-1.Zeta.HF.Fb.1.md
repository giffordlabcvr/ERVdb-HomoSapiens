# ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fb.1 (HERV-Fb)

**ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fb.1 (HERV-Fb)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the HERV-F-related radiation of primate gammaretroviruses. Comparative analyses indicate that the lineage entered the primate germ line during the Eocene or earlier.

Human HERV-Fb loci are represented by relatively few, highly degraded proviruses, and no replication-competent representatives are known. The lineage is best known as the source of the domesticated **Suppressyn** (*SUPYN*) gene, although most HERV-Fb loci have accumulated extensive mutations since their integration.

Together with **HERV-Fa** and **HERV-XA**, HERV-Fb forms part of a broader radiation of phenylalanine primer-binding-site (PBS) retroviruses that circulated among ancestral primates tens of millions of years ago.
