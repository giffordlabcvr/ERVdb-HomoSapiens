# ERV-1.Zeta.FRD.66 (MER-66)

**ERV-1.Zeta.FRD.66 (MER-66)** is an ancient endogenous gammaretrovirus lineage belonging to the proposed **Zeta** genus within the **Clade I** radiation of human ERVs. Phylogenetically, it forms part of the **HERV-FRD-like** group, a cluster of related lineages that includes HERV-FRD and several other deeply diverged gammaretroviral groups. The human genome contains only a small number of highly degraded MER66 proviral loci together with associated fragmented repeat sequences.
