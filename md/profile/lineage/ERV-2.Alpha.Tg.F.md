# ERV-2.Alpha.Tg.F (TgERV-F)

**ERV-2.Alpha.Tg.F** (TgERV-F) is an endogenous retrovirus lineage identified in passerine birds, including the zebra finch (*Taeniopygia guttata*). Multiple near-complete proviruses and hundreds of solo LTRs have been identified in the zebra finch genome, while related elements occur in several other bird species, indicating that the lineage remained active over an extended period of avian evolution (PMID: 24352464).

TgERV-F is notable for its recombinant origin. Phylogenetic analyses place its *gag* and *pol* genes among avian clade II retroviruses related to alpharetroviruses, whereas its *env* gene is most closely related to mammalian gammaretroviruses. This discordance indicates that the ancestral virus acquired its envelope gene through recombination with a distantly related retrovirus.
