# ERV-2.Beta.K.HML.7 (HERV-K-HML7)

**ERV-2.Beta.K.HML.7 (HERV-K-HML7)** is an ancient endogenous betaretrovirus lineage belonging to the **Kappa (K)** sublineage of betaretroviruses (genus *Betaretrovirus*). The human genome contains **23 proviral loci** together with approximately **160 solo long terminal repeats (LTRs)**. HML7 forms a single well-supported monophyletic lineage with no obvious subdivision into major subgroups. HML7 insertions show a significant enrichment on **chromosome X**, and nearly half of the proviral loci occur within introns of human genes.

Comparative genomic analyses indicate that HML7 colonised catarrhine primate genomes from the late Eocene through the early Miocene, with the principal wave of integrations occurring during the late Oligocene and early Miocene (approximately 30–20 million years ago) (PMID: 35676699).
