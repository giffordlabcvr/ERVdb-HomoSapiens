# ERV-2.Beta.Archaeo.5 (HERV-K-HML5)

**ERV-2.Beta.Archaeo.5**, also known as *human endogenous retrovirus K HML5 (HERV-K-HML5)* is the oldest known betaretroviral lineage within the primate germ line. Comparative analyses indicate that HML-5 entered the simian germ line >55 million years ago (the early Eocene), prior to the  divergence of New World and Old World monkeys.

Although historically classified within the ‘HERV-K’ group, phylogenetic analyses show it is distinct from the main cluster of HERV-K ERV lineages found in primate genomes.
