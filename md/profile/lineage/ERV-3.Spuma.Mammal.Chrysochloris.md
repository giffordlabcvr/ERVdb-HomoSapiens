# ERV-3.Spuma.Mammal.Chrysochloris (ChrEFV)

**ERV-3.Spuma.Mammal.Chrysochloris (ChrEFV)** is an endogenous foamy virus lineage identified in the Cape golden mole (*Chrysochloris asiatica*). It is represented by a single partial proviral sequence and was the first foamy virus reported from an afrotherian mammal, extending the known host range of foamy viruses to all major placental mammal groups.

Phylogenetically, ChrEFV occupies a deep position within the mammalian foamy virus radiation, consistent with the early divergence of Afrotheria. However, because the lineage is represented by a highly degraded endogenous sequence, its precise evolutionary position remains uncertain.

ChrEFV contributes to growing evidence that foamy viruses have circulated among mammals for tens of millions of years and highlights the value of endogenous viral elements for reconstructing the early evolution of the **Spumavirus** genus.
