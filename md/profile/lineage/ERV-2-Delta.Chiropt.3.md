# ERV-2-Delta.Chiropt.3

**ERV-2-Delta.Chiropt.3** is an endogenous deltaretrovirus lineage identified in the little tube-nosed bat (*Murina aurata*). The lineage is represented by a near-complete provirus retaining substantial portions of the **gag**, **pro** and **pol** genes together with paired long terminal repeats (LTRs).

MurMur is notable for preserving part of a recognizable *tax* homolog. Together with **MinERVa**, this provides evidence that the characteristic regulatory gene repertoire of complex deltaretroviruses had already evolved tens of millions of years before the emergence of modern **HTLV** and **BLV** lineages.

MurMur forms part of a growing collection of endogenous deltaretroviruses identified in bats, supporting the view that the historical diversity and host range of the genus were substantially greater than suggested by extant viruses alone.
