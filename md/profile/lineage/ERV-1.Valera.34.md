# ERV-1.Valera.34 (MER-34)

**ERV-1.Valera.34 (MER-34)** is an ancient clade I endogenous retrovirus lineage that invaded the germ line of early placental mammals during the Cretaceous Period, more than 100 million years ago. Comparative genomic analyses indicate that MER-34 entered mammalian germ lines before the diversification of many modern placental mammal groups (PMID: 28739914).

The MER-34 lineage is the source of HEMO, a host gene derived from a captured retroviral envelope (env) gene. HEMO is secreted into the maternal circulation during pregnancy and is also expressed in pluripotent stem cells and several tumour types.
