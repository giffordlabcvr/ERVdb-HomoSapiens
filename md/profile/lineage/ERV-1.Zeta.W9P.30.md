# ERV-1.Zeta.W9P.30 (HERV-30)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9P.30 (HERV.30)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the **W9** radiation of primate gammaretroviruses. It is most closely related to **HERV-W**, **HERV-9** and **HERV-P**, but is distinguished by a characteristic family of long terminal repeats (LTRs).

Comparative analyses indicate that HERV.30 entered the primate germ line during the Eocene or earlier. Human HERV.30 loci are represented by relatively few, highly degraded proviruses, and no replication-competent representatives or domesticated host genes are known.
