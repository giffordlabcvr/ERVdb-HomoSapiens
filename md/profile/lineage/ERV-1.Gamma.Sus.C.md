# ERV-1.Gamma.Sus.C (PERV-C)

**ERV-1.Gamma.Sus.C** (PERV-C) is an endogenous gammaretrovirus lineage found in domestic pigs and other suids. Together with PERV-A and PERV-B, it forms the porcine endogenous retrovirus (PERV) family. Unlike most endogenous retrovirus lineages, PERV-C remains replication competent in many pig populations and is capable of producing infectious virus.

PERV-C became the focus of extensive study because of concerns surrounding xenotransplantation. Although PERV-C itself is ecotropic and infects only pig cells, recombination with the human-tropic lineage PERV-A can generate **PERV-A/C** recombinant viruses that infect human cells efficiently in vitro and typically replicate more effectively than either parental lineage. Experimental studies have also shown that relatively few amino acid substitutions in the PERV-C envelope protein are sufficient to permit entry into human cells.

These findings made the presence of PERV-C an important consideration in the selection and engineering of pigs for xenotransplantation.

So far, no confirmed transmission of PERVs to humans has been documented following clinical or experimental xenotransplantation.
