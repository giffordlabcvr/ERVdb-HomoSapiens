# ERV-1.Gamma.RD114 (RD114)

**ERV-1.Gamma.RD114 (RD114)** is a feline endogenous gammaretrovirus lineage originally identified after the recovery of an infectious retrovirus from a human rhabdomyosarcoma cell line. Subsequent studies showed that the virus was not of human origin but instead arose from endogenous retroviral sequences present in the domestic cat genome.

RD114 is the prototype of the **RD114/type D interference group**, whose members use the neutral amino acid transporter **ASCT2 (SLC1A5)** as a cellular receptor. Its envelope protein has been widely used to pseudotype retroviral vectors because of its broad cellular tropism.

Although no intact RD114 provirus corresponding to the classical virus exists in the domestic cat genome, genomic analyses demonstrated that infectious RD114-like viruses can be regenerated through recombination between defective endogenous loci. This finding resolved a longstanding question regarding the origin of infectious RD114 isolates and highlighted the potential for endogenous retroviral loci to generate novel viruses through recombination.
