# ERV-2-Beta.Mys.TR

**ERV-2.Beta.Mys.TR (mysTR)** is a highly amplified endogenous betaretrovirus lineage found in several species of sigmodontine rodents. In some species, mysTR has expanded to approximately 10,000 genomic copies, making it one of the most abundant endogenous retrovirus-derived lineages known in mammals.

Near-complete mysTR proviruses retain recognizable **gag**, **pro** and **pol** genes but lack an **env** gene. As a result, mysTR is thought to propagate exclusively within the genome by retrotransposition rather than by producing infectious virus. Independent expansions in different rodent species indicate that this retrotransposon-like lifestyle has been highly successful over evolutionary time.

mysTR was discovered during studies of South American rodents that had lost **LINE-1** retrotransposon activity. Although a causal relationship has not been established, the coincidence has prompted interest in possible interactions between different classes of genomic retroelements during genome evolution.
