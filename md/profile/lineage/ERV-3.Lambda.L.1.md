# ERV-3.Lambda.L.1 (ERV-L)

**ERV-3.Lambda.L.1 (ERV-L)** is one of the oldest endogenous retrovirus lineages found in mammalian genomes. It appears to occur throughout placental mammals, and includes loci that predate their divergence, indicating that the lineage was already established before the major mammalian radiations of the Cenozoic. All known HERV-L loci lack an *env* gene, indicating that genomic expansion occurred largely through intracellular retrotransposition.
