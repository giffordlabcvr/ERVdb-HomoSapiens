# ERV-3.Lambda.U3.1 (HERV-U3)

**ERV-3.Lambda.U3.1**, also known as *human endogenous retrovirus type U3 (HERV-U3)* is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the Lambda group. Unlike most members of the broader HERV-L radiation, HERV-U3 retains a portion of the envelope (*env*) gene, allowing it to be recognised through transmembrane (TM)-based screens for endogenous retroviral envelope proteins.
