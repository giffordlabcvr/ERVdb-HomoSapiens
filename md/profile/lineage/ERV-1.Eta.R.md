# ERV-1.Eta.R (HERV-R)

**ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.R**, also known as *human endogenous retrovirus type R (HERV-R)* is a lineage of ancient ERV1 gammaretroviruses that entered the genomes of Catarrhine primates after the divergence of Old World and New World monkeys. The lineage is best known through the ERV3 proviral locus, one of the earliest human endogenous retroviral loci to be described and one of the most extensively studied ERV loci in the human genome.
