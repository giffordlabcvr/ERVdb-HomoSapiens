# ERV-1.Rho.Rb.1 (HERV-Rb)

**ERV-1.Rho.Rb.1**, also known as *human endogenous retrovirus type Rb (HERV-Rb)** and *PABL*, is an ancient clade I endogenous retrovirus lineage found in primates. Orthologous insertions indicate that the lineage entered the primate germ line during the Eocene or earlier.

Most HERV-Rb loci are highly degraded and no replication-competent representatives are known. The lineage is best known as the source of **ERVPABLB-1** (*envR(b)*), an envelope-derived gene that has been preserved by natural selection long after the extinction of the ancestral virus. Although the biological function of envR(b) remains incompletely understood, its long-term conservation indicates that it performs an important host function.
