# ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA.1 (HERV-XA)

**ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA.1 (HERV-XA)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the HERV-F-related radiation of primate gammaretroviruses. The lineage is closely related to **HERV-Fa** and **HERV-Fb**, together forming a group of phenylalanine primer-binding-site (PBS) retroviruses.

Comparative analyses indicate that HERV-XA entered the primate germ line during the Eocene or earlier. Although represented by relatively few loci in the human genome, related elements occur in both New World and Old World monkeys, indicating that the lineage was established before the major simian radiations.

No host genes have been convincingly shown to derive from the HERV-XA lineage, although some loci remain transcriptionally active. Human HERV-XA proviruses are highly degraded and no replication-competent representatives are known.
