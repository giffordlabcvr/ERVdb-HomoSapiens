# ERV-1.Zeta.HF.H.1-10 (ERVH-10)

**ERV-1.Zeta.HF.H.1-10** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.HF.H.1** lineage. It corresponds to the locus assigned the HGNC gene symbol **ERVH-10**, historically known as **envH3** or **envH/p59**, reflecting the retention of an expressed envelope (**env**) gene.

ERVH-10 is expressed in several human tissues, including the placenta, and encodes one of the relatively small number of HERV-derived proteins recognised by the HGNC. Although its envelope protein has been investigated as a potential placenta-associated gene product, no definitive physiological function has yet been established, and it has not been shown to possess the cell-fusion activity characteristic of the syncytins.

ERV-1.Zeta.HF.H.1-10 illustrates the long-term retention of retroviral envelope genes in the human genome and is one of several HERV loci that have been assigned official HGNC gene symbols because of their transcriptional and protein-coding potential.
