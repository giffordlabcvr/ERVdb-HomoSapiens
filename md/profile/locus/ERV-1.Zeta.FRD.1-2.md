# ERV-1.Zeta.FRD.1-2 (Syncytin-2)

**ERV-1.Zeta.FRD.1-2** is the human endogenous retrovirus locus that encodes **Syncytin-2**, an envelope (*env*)-derived protein essential for normal placental development. Unlike most endogenous retroviral loci, which have accumulated disabling mutations, this insertion has retained an intact *env* open reading frame that has been maintained by purifying selection since its capture in the common ancestor of catarrhine primates.

Syncytin-2 is expressed in placental trophoblast cells, where it mediates the fusion of cytotrophoblasts to form the multinucleated syncytiotrophoblast. This cell layer forms the principal interface between the developing fetus and the maternal circulation and is essential for normal placental function. The ERV-1.Zeta.FRD.1-2 locus is one of the best-studied examples of molecular exaptation, demonstrating how an ancient retroviral envelope gene has been adapted to perform an essential function in mammalian development.
