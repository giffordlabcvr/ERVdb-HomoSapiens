# Alpharetrovirus

**Alpharetrovirus** is a formally recognised genus within the subfamily **Orthoretrovirinae** and is assigned to **Clade II** in ERVdb. The genus is represented principally by avian retroviruses, including Rous sarcoma virus and the avian leukosis viruses. ERVdb also places lymphoproliferative disease virus within this group.

Alpharetroviruses are best known as exogenous viruses of birds, in which they can cause neoplastic and other diseases. Endogenous alpharetroviral sequences in avian genomes extend the documented history and diversity of the group. These include **TgERV-F**, an alpharetroviral lineage reconstructed from the genome of the zebra finch (*Taeniopygia guttata*).
