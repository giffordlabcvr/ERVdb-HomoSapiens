# Betaretrovirus: ARCHAEO

**ARCHAEO** is an ERVdb-defined subgroup of *Betaretrovirus* within *Clade II*. It is treated as the sister grouping to K in the current ERVdb phylogenetic framework and is presently represented by human endogenous retroviruses.

Earlier lineage metadata associates *HERV-K-HML5* and *HERV-K-HML6* with the corresponding archaeo-beta grouping. The boundary between ARCHAEO and K remains under review as additional candidate lineages are examined.
