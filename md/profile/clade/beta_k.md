# Betaretrovirus: K

**K** is an ERVdb-defined subgroup of *Betaretrovirus* within *Clade II*. It contains both human and non-human endogenous retroviral representatives and is treated separately from its sister grouping, ARCHAEO.

The human component includes multiple HERV-K HML lineages. In the earlier lineage metadata, K encompasses HML1–4 and HML7–10, while HML5 and HML6 are assigned to the archaeo-beta grouping. This framework distinguishes evolutionary groupings within the broader collection of elements historically described as HERV-K.
