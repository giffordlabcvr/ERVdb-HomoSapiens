# Valera

**Valera** is an ERVdb-defined genus-level grouping that includes the human MER34 lineage and related endogenous retroviral representatives from another mammal and an alligator. It is not currently recognised as a formal taxon by the ICTV.

Its current composition brings together mammalian and reptilian representatives within a single grouping. This distribution extends the comparative context of MER34 beyond the human genome, while leaving the timing and direction of historical host associations unresolved.
