# Iotaretrovirus: avian grouping

The **avian Iota grouping** organises bird-derived endogenous retroviral representatives assigned to *Iotaretrovirus* in ERVdb. These sequences extend the representation of Iota beyond the mammalian lineages that include human *ERV-I* and *ERV-ADP*.

The grouping provides a host-associated alignment unit for comparing the avian representatives within the wider Clade I Iota framework.
