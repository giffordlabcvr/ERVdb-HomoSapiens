Epsilonretrovirus
=================

**Epsilonretrovirus** is a formally recognised genus within the subfamily **Orthoretrovirinae** and is assigned to **Clade I** in ERVdb. The genus is represented by fish retroviruses, including walleye dermal sarcoma virus and walleye epidermal hyperplasia viruses 1 and 2.

Epsilonretroviruses are associated with proliferative skin diseases in fish. The best-characterised members infect walleye, in which virus-associated dermal sarcomas and epidermal hyperplasias develop and regress seasonally. Epsilonretroviruses possess accessory genes in addition to the canonical retroviral genes; these include an **rv-cyclin** gene implicated in the regulation of transcription and cell growth.

As in other well-characterised members of Clade I, epsilonretroviruses use translational readthrough to regulate expression of *pol*. Suppression of the termination codon following *gag* permits production of a Gag--Pol polyprotein, while termination produces Gag alone at greater abundance.
