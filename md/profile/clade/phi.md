# Phi

**Phi** is an ERVdb-defined genus-level grouping of endogenous retroviruses assigned to *Clade III*. Its currently identified members are avian. The name is used within the ERVdb classification framework and is not an ICTV-recognised genus.

Additional candidate members have been identified beyond the representative currently included in ERVdb. The relationship between Phi and the avian Chi grouping remains under investigation, so their treatment as separate groups is provisional.
