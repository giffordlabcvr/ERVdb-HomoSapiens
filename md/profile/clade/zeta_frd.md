# Zetaretrovirus: FRD

**FRD** is an ERVdb-defined grouping within *Zetaretrovirus*, assigned to *Clade I*. It includes the human *HERV-FRD* lineage and additional human and non-human endogenous retroviral representatives.

The grouping accommodates Zeta diversity whose deeper relationships are less clearly resolved than those of the HF and W9P radiations. It is used as a working comparative unit rather than as a claim that all currently assigned lineages form a securely resolved monophyletic subgroup.
