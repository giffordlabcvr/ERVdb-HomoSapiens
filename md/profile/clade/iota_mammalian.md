# Iotaretrovirus: mammalian grouping

The **mammalian Iota grouping** organises the mammalian endogenous retroviruses assigned to *Iotaretrovirus* in ERVdb. Human representatives include the *ERV-I* and *ERV-ADP* lineages, alongside representatives from other mammals.

This host-associated grouping provides the tip-level alignment framework for comparisons among the mammalian representatives. Its name identifies the hosts of the included sequences and does not, by itself, establish the historical host range of the ancestral viruses.
