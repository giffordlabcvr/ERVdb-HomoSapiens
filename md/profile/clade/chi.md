# Chi

**Chi** is an ERVdb-defined genus-level grouping of avian endogenous retroviruses assigned to *Clade III*. The name is used within the ERVdb classification framework and is not an ICTV-recognised genus.

Chi is distinguished provisionally from Phi, another avian Clade III grouping. Their separation is under review, and the present classification should be interpreted as a working division of the avian sequences represented in the ERVdb phylogeny.
