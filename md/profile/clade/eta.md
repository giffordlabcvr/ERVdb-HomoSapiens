# Etaretrovirus

*Etaretrovirus* is an ERVdb-defined genus-level grouping assigned to *Clade I*. It is distinct from the formally recognised genus *Gammaretrovirus* and is not currently recognised as a formal taxon by the ICTV.

The group includes endogenous retroviral lineages from mammals and reptiles, including lineages represented in the human genome. In the ERVdb phylogenetic framework, Eta and Gamma form neighbouring branches within the terrestrial vertebrate radiation of Clade I. Their separation distinguishes an additional component of gamma-related retroviral diversity that is represented in the endogenous fossil record.
