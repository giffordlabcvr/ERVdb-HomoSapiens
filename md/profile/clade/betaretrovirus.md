Betaretrovirus
==============

*Betaretrovirus* is a formally recognised genus within the subfamily *Orthoretrovirinae* and is assigned to *Clade II* in ERVdb. Representative exogenous members include mouse mammary tumour virus, Mason-Pfizer monkey virus, jaagsiekte sheep retrovirus and squirrel monkey retrovirus. ERVdb divides the genus into the *A*, *Archaeobeta*, *B*, *D* and *K* groups, reflecting major phylogenetic divisions within the broader betaretroviral radiation.

Betaretroviruses are best known from mammals, in which exogenous members are associated with neoplastic, respiratory and immunosuppressive diseases. The endogenous record is exceptionally diverse and extends beyond the host range represented by known exogenous viruses. It includes the intracisternal A-type particle elements of rodents, betaretroviral lineages in numerous other mammals, and related elements in reptiles. In humans, the genus is represented by the Kappa (K) lineage, which includes HERV-K groups, including the relatively young HERV-K(HML-2) lineage, together with the more ancient Archaeobeta lineage.
