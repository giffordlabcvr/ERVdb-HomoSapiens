Lambdaretrovirus
================

**Lambdaretrovirus** is an ERVdb-defined genus of exclusively endogenous retroviruses assigned to **Clade III**. It is not currently recognised as a formal taxon by the ICTV. Representative lineages include human **ERV-L** and **ERV-U3**, murine **MuERV-L**, and the equine **EqERV-U3** lineage.

Lambdaretroviruses are widely distributed in mammalian genomes and represent an ancient component of the retroviral fossil record. Some ERV-L insertions are shared across widely separated placental mammal groups, demonstrating that members of the genus were already present before the early diversification of placental mammals. The group has no known exogenous representatives, and its biological properties must therefore be inferred from reconstructed ancestral sequences and surviving genomic copies.
