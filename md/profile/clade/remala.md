# Remala

**Remala** is an ERVdb-defined genus-level grouping of avian endogenous retroviruses assigned to *Clade I*. It is not currently recognised as a formal taxon by the ICTV.

In the ERVdb reference phylogeny, Remala branches near the base of the terrestrial vertebrate assemblage containing Gamma, Eta, Rho and Zeta. It is treated separately from Rho, with which these avian elements were previously associated during curation. The grouping therefore distinguishes an avian branch within the deeper structure of terrestrial Clade I retrovirus diversity.
