# ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA-1 (ERVFH21-1)

**ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA-1** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA** lineage. It corresponds to the locus assigned the HGNC gene symbol **ERVFH21-1** and was historically identified as **HERV-F(XA34)** (PMID: 8760409). This locus has been studied because it is transcriptionally active and was among the earliest HERV-F-related insertions to be molecularly characterised.

ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA-1 is located on chromosome 7 and comprises a substantially complete proviral structure flanked by long terminal repeats (LTRs). Although it retains recognisable *gag*, *pro*, *pol* and *env* genes, all have accumulated mutations during their residence in the human genome and the provirus is not considered capable of producing an infectious virus. The *env* gene contains a large internal deletion together with additional frameshifts and premature stop codons, while *pol* is also disrupted. Despite this structural degradation, the locus preserves much of the ancestral proviral organisation, making it one of the best-characterised representatives of the ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA lineage.

The locus is transcribed in several human tissues, including the placenta, and multiple transcript isoforms have been described. Early studies identified spliced transcripts that remove much of the internal proviral sequence while retaining a small open reading frame near the 3′ end. Although these transcripts have been proposed to possess biological activity, no definitive physiological function has yet been established.

Historically, the sequence recovered from this locus was designated **HERV-F(XA34)** and subsequently served as the reference sequence for the RepBase entry **ERVFH21-1**. As a result, the names *HERV-F(XA34)* and *ERVFH21-1* have been used in the literature both for this individual genomic locus and, by extension, for the broader ERV lineage. In ERVdb these concepts are distinguished: **ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA** refers to the endogenous retrovirus lineage, whereas **ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA-1** designates this specific proviral insertion.

Because of its relatively complete structure, documented transcription and long history of investigation, ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA-1 remains the best-studied human representative of the ERV-1.Zeta.HF.XA lineage.
