# ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fc.2-1 (ERVFC1-1)

**ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fc.2-1** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fc.2** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVFC1-1** and has previously been annotated as **FLJ42291**, **envF(c)2**, and **TCONS_l2_00027524**.

The locus retains an expressed envelope (*env*) gene and is one of a small number of endogenous retroviral loci assigned an official HGNC gene symbol. Although transcription of **ERVFC1-1** has been reported, its biological function remains poorly understood and no definitive physiological role has yet been established.
