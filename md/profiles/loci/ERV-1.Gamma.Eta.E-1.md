# ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.E-1 (ERVE-1)

**ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.E-1** (HGNC symbol **ERVE-1**) is a HERV-E-derived proviral insertion located on chromosome 17. Unlike most endogenous retroviral loci, it has been assigned an HGNC gene symbol and is annotated as a long non-coding RNA (lncRNA). Transcription is initiated from the proviral long terminal repeat (LTR), illustrating how endogenous retroviral regulatory sequences can become integrated into host gene expression networks.

ERVE-1 is expressed in several normal tissues, including the pancreas, thyroid and placenta. Its LTR promoter exhibits tissue-specific DNA methylation, being relatively hypomethylated in placental cells but more heavily methylated in blood and other somatic tissues, suggesting epigenetic regulation of transcription.

Although ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.E-1 has no known protein-coding capacity, its RNA transcript and LTR have attracted interest as potential regulators of host gene expression. Altered ERVE-1 expression has been reported in autoimmune diseases including systemic lupus erythematosus and multiple sclerosis, although its biological function and any role in disease remain uncertain.
