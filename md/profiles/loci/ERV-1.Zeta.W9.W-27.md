# ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-27 (ERVW-27)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-27** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVW-27**, one of several HERV-W-derived loci assigned official gene symbols. Although the locus retains recognisable retroviral features and is transcribed, no specific biological function has yet been established. ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-27 contributes to the diversity of HERV-W loci retained in the human genome.
