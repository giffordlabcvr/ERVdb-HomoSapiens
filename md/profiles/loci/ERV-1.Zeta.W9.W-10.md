# ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-10 (ERVW-10)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-10** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W** lineage. 
It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVW-10**, one of several HERV-W-derived loci assigned official gene symbols. 
Although the locus retains recognisable retroviral features and is transcribed, no specific biological function has yet been established.
