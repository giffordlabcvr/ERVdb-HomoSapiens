# ERV-1.Theta.34-1 (HEMO)

**ERV-1.Theta.34-1** is a human MER34-derived endogenous retrovirus locus located on chromosome 4. Unlike most MER-34 insertions, it has retained an intact envelope (*env*)-derived open reading frame that was co-opted during mammalian evolution to encode **HEMO** (Human Endogenous MER34 ORF).

HEMO is expressed predominantly in the placenta, where it is secreted into the maternal circulation by trophoblast cells. It is also expressed in pluripotent stem cells and several tumour types, suggesting roles in developmental processes that remain incompletely understood. Unlike the better-known syncytin proteins, HEMO has not been shown to mediate cell-cell fusion, instead functioning as a secreted envelope-derived protein. Its long-term evolutionary conservation illustrates how an ancient endogenous retroviral gene has been adapted for host biology.
