# ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.1 (HERV-1)

**ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.1** (historically **HERV-1**) is an endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the proposed **Eta** genus within the **gammaretrovirus** radiation (Clade I). Although historically regarded as closely related to **HERV-R**, phylogenetic analyses support its recognition as a distinct lineage with its own characteristic long terminal repeat (LTR) sequences, indicating that the two groups derive from separate ancestral viruses.

Like other members of the Eta genus, HERV-1 represents an ancient germline invasion that has survived only as fragmented and highly degraded proviral remnants within the human genome. No intact coding sequences or co-opted host genes have been identified, and the lineage has not been associated with any specific biological function or disease.

HERV-1 has received relatively little attention compared with other human endogenous retrovirus lineages, and its evolutionary relationships have remained obscured by the inconsistent historical use of HERV nomenclature. Recognition of HERV-1 as a distinct lineage helps resolve this historical ambiguity and more accurately reflects the evolutionary diversity of the Eta radiation, which also includes HERV-E, HERV-R, HERV-15 and related mammalian endogenous retroviruses.
