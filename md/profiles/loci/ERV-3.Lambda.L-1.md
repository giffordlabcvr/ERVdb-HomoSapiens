# ERV-3.Lambda.L-1 (ERV18-1)

**ERV-3.Lambda.L-1** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-3.Lambda.L** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERV18-1**. Apart from its annotation as an endogenous retroviral locus, little is currently known about its biological significance.
