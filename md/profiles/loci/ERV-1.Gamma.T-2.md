# ERV-1.Gamma.T-2 (ERVS71-2)

**ERV-1.Gamma.T-2** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Gamma.T** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVS71-2** and contains the only known HERV-T envelope (**env**) gene to have retained an intact coding sequence in hominids.

Experimental reconstruction of the ancestral HERV-T envelope demonstrated that HERV-T used the cell-surface transporter **MCT-1 (SLC16A1)** as its cellular receptor. Although the modern ERVS71-2 envelope protein can no longer mediate viral entry, it retains the ability to bind MCT-1 and promotes its removal from the cell surface, thereby blocking infection by viruses bearing the reconstructed ancestral HERV-T envelope.

ERV-1.Gamma.T-2 therefore represents a striking example of endogenous retroviral gene co-option. The preserved envelope gene appears to have been repurposed as an antiviral restriction factor, raising the possibility that it contributed to the extinction of HERV-T as an infectious virus during hominid evolution.
