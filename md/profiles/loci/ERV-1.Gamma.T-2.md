# ERV-1.Gamma.T-2 (ERVS71-2)

**ERV-1.Gamma.T-2** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Gamma.T** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVS71-2** and was historically designated **HERV-HC2**.

Unlike **ERVS71-1**, which preserves an intact HERV-T envelope gene, ERVS71-2 has not been associated with a specific biological function. It represents a distinct HERV-T-derived locus recognised by HGNC, but little is currently known about its physiological significance.
