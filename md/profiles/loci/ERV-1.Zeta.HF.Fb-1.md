# ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fb-1 (Suppressyn / SUPYN)

The human locus **ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fb-1**, commonly known as **Suppressyn (SUPYN)**, is a domesticated *env* gene derived from the HERV-Fb lineage. Orthologous loci shared by catarrhine primates indicate that the ancestral HERV-Fb provirus entered the germ line before the divergence of Old World monkeys and apes, after which the *env* gene was maintained by purifying selection while the remainder of the provirus accumulated disabling mutations.

Suppressyn is expressed in placental tissues and encodes a truncated retroviral envelope protein that binds the neutral amino acid transporter **ASCT2 (SLC1A5)**. Experimental studies have shown that Suppressyn inhibits ASCT2-mediated entry by RD114- and type D-related retroviruses, supporting the proposal that it functions as an antiviral restriction factor.

Suppressyn represents a distinct outcome of retroviral gene domestication. Whereas the independently acquired syncytin proteins retained the membrane fusion activity of retroviral envelope proteins to support placental development, Suppressyn appears to have evolved primarily as a receptor interference factor that protects host cells from infection by related exogenous retroviruses.
