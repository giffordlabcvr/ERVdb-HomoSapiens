# ERV-1.Zeta.W9P.W-8 (ERVW-8)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9P.W-8** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.W9P.W** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVW-8**, one of several HERV-W-derived loci assigned official gene symbols. Although the locus retains recognisable retroviral features and is transcribed, no specific biological function has yet been established.
