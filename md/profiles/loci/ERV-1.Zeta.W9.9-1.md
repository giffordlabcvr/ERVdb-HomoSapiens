# ERV-1.Zeta.W9.9-1 (ERV9-1)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9.9-1** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.W9.9** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERV9-1** and was historically designated **pTR2**, one of the first transcriptionally active ERV9 loci to be characterised.

The **pTR2** locus contributed to the identification of the ERV9 family and to early studies demonstrating that ERV9 long terminal repeats (LTRs) can function as promoters for host gene transcription. While the regulatory properties of ERV9 LTRs have been investigated extensively, no specific physiological function has been established for the ERV9-1 locus itself.
