# ERV-2.Beta.K.HML2-101 (ERVK-24)

**ERV-2.Beta.K.HML2-101** is a human-specific HERV-K (HML-2) proviral locus located upstream of the **PRODH** (*proline dehydrogenase*) gene on chromosome 22q11.21. Also known as **ERVK-24**, **HERV-K101**, and **c22_A**, this insertion has been shown to function as a tissue-specific enhancer that contributes to the regulation of **PRODH** expression.

Experimental studies have demonstrated that the proviral long terminal repeat (LTR) enhances **PRODH** transcription through binding of the transcription factor **SOX2**, acting synergistically with regulatory elements within the **PRODH** locus. Enhancer activity is regulated by DNA methylation and is particularly associated with neuronal expression in the hippocampus. Comparative analyses indicate that the insertion is absent from the orthologous chimpanzee locus, suggesting that it represents a human-specific regulatory innovation.

Because **PRODH** has been implicated in several neurodevelopmental and neuropsychiatric disorders, including schizophrenia, **ERV-2.Beta.K.HML2-101** has attracted interest as a potential contributor to human brain biology. However, current evidence supports a role in regulating **PRODH** expression rather than establishing a direct causal relationship between the ERV insertion and disease.
