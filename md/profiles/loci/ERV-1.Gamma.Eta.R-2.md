# ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.R-2 (ERV3-2)

**ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.R-2** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Gamma.R** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERV3-2** and was previously annotated as **KIAA1466** before its endogenous retroviral origin was recognised.

The locus retains a protein-coding sequence derived from the ancestral retroviral genome and is one of a small number of HERV loci assigned an official HGNC gene symbol. Although transcripts have been reported, its biological function remains poorly understood and has not been characterised to the same extent as the related **ERV-1.Gamma.R-1 (ERV3-1)** locus.
