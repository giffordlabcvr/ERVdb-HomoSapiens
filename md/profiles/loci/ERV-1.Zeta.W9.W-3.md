# ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-3 (ERVW-3)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-3** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVW-3**, historically also referred to as **CL4**. 
Apart from its annotation as a HERV-W locus, little is currently known about its biological significance.
