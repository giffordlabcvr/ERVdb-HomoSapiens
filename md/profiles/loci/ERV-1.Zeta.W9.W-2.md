# ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-2 (ERVW-2)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-2** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVW-2** and is historically known as **NTrenv**, reflecting its encoded envelope (**env**) gene.

Unlike the closely related **ERV-1.Zeta.W9.W-1 (ERVW-1)** locus, which encodes the placental fusogen Syncytin-1, the envelope protein encoded by **ERVW-2** has not been shown to possess cell-fusion activity. Although the gene is transcribed and has been investigated as a potential functional envelope protein, no definitive physiological role has yet been established.
