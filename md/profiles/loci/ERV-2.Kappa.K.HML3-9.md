# ERV-2.Kappa.K.HML3-9 (ERVK9-9)

**ERV-2.Kappa.K.HML3-9** is a human endogenous retrovirus locus belonging to the **ERV-2.Kappa.K.HML3** lineage. It corresponds to the HGNC gene **ERVK9-9** and was historically designated **HERV-K(HML-3) K18B**.

Apart from its recognition as a distinct HML-3-derived endogenous retroviral locus, little is currently known about its biological significance. The locus has been assigned an official HGNC gene symbol, but no specific physiological function has yet been established.

ERV-2.Kappa.K.HML3-9 is representative of the many HML-3 loci that contribute to the diversity of the human endogenous retrovirus repertoire while remaining only sparsely characterised.
