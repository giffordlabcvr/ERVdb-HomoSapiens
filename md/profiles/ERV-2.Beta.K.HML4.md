# HERV-K(HML-3)

**HERV-K(HML-3)** is a primate endogenous retrovirus family within the HERV-K (human MMTV-like; HML) supergroup of betaretrovirus-related elements. In the human genome it is represented by **~140 proviral loci or remnants per haploid genome**, broadly distributed across chromosomes. Only a small minority of loci retain near full-length proviral structure with LTRs on both sides; most are degraded by mutation and large internal deletions. Two recurrent structural variants are common: a **gag-region deletion** (often extending into protease) and a **pol-region deletion** that removes reverse transcriptase/RNase H domains, sometimes occurring together in the same locus. A family consensus reconstructed from multiple loci is **~7.9 kb** with ~512 bp LTRs and encodes uninterrupted ORFs for **gag, prt, pol, and env**; notably, the protease ORF includes a **dUTPase domain** with conserved motifs consistent with ancestral functionality. The **env** region shows multiple insertion/deletion variants that alter predicted Env length and coding capacity, including truncated gp36-like transmembrane features. Phylogenies show near-monophyly with two closely related subgroups, and LTR divergence indicates integration **~36 million years ago**, with no evidence of sustained activity thereafter.
