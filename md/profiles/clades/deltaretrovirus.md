Deltaretrovirus
===============

*Deltaretrovirus* is a formally recognised genus within the subfamily *Orthoretrovirinae* and is assigned to *Clade II* in ERVdb. Representative exogenous members include bovine leukaemia virus and the primate T-lymphotropic viruses, including human T-lymphotropic viruses 1 and 2. ERVdb divides the genus into *cetartiodactyl*, *chiropteran* and *primate*groups, reflecting the principal host-associated divisions currently represented within the deltaretroviral radiation.

Deltaretroviruses infect mammals and include viruses associated with leukaemia, lymphoma and other lymphoproliferative diseases. They are complex retroviruses and possess regulatory and accessory genes in addition to the canonical retroviral genes. These include *tax* and *rex*, which regulate viral transcription and RNA expression, together with additional genes that vary among deltaretroviral groups.

Although deltaretroviruses were historically known principally from exogenous viruses of primates and cattle, endogenous deltaretroviral sequences have revealed a broader host range and deeper evolutionary history. ERVdb includes endogenous lineages from bats and a river dolphin, while an apparently exogenous deltaretrovirus has also been identified in bats. These discoveries indicate that deltaretroviruses have circulated among divergent mammalian groups over an extended period.
