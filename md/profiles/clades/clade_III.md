Clade III
=========

**Clade III** is one of three major retroviral groups recognised in ERVdb. It comprises the formally recognised subfamily *Spumaretrovirinae*, including foamy viruses and related endogenous forms, together with the exclusively endogenous, ERVdb-defined *Lambdaretrovirus* and *Sigmaretrovirus* groups. Clade III represents a deep phylogenetic grouping rather than a formal ICTV taxon and does not correspond directly to the current division of Retroviridae into subfamilies.

Clade III retroviruses have been identified in a wide range of vertebrate hosts. Foamy viruses circulate exogenously in several mammalian groups, while endogenous spumaretroviruses extend the documented history and host range of the group to other mammals and non-mammalian vertebrates, including reptiles, amphibians and coelacanths. The Lambda and Sigma groups are known exclusively from ERVs and include several ancient mammalian lineages, among them the human ERV-L, ERV-U3 and ERV-S groups. The ERV record therefore reveals a substantially greater diversity within Clade III than is represented by its known exogenous members.

Clade III also encompasses contrasting strategies for the expression of *pol*. Members of the Lambda and Sigma groups appear to use translational readthrough to express *pol* as part of a Gag-Pol polyprotein. Foamy viruses instead express *pol*independently, rather than primarily as a Gag-Pol fusion.
