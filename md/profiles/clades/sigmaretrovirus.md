Sigmaretrovirus
===============

*Sigmaretrovirus* is an ERVdb-defined genus of exclusively endogenous retroviruses assigned to *Clade III*. It is not currently recognised as a formal taxon by the ICTV. The genus is represented in ERVdb by the human **ERV-S** lineage and the equine **EqERV-S** lineage.

Sigmaretroviruses are known only from mammalian genomic sequences, and no exogenous representative has been identified. Their phylogenetic placement distinguishes them from the related lambdaretroviruses and from the spumavirus radiation. The limited number of characterised lineages means that the former host range, diversity and biological properties of the group remain poorly understood.
