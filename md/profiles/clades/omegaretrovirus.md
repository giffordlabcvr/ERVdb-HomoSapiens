# Thetaretrovirus

*Thetaretrovirus* is an ERVdb-defined genus of exclusively endogenous retroviruses assigned to *Clade I*. It is not currently recognised as a formal taxon by the ICTV. The genus is represented in ERVdb by the human *ERV-Lb*, *ERV-24* and *MER34* lineages.

Thetaretroviruses are known principally from ancient retroviral sequences in primate genomes, and no exogenous representative has been identified. Phylogenetic analysis supports their recognition as a distinct early-diverging radiation within Clade I, separate from the gammaretroviral and zetaretroviral groups.
