Clade II
========

*Clade II* is one of three major retroviral groups recognised in ERVdb. It comprises the formally recognised genera *Alpharetrovirus*, *Betaretrovirus*, *Deltaretrovirus* and *Lentivirus*, all of which are currently classified within the subfamily *Orthoretrovirinae*. Clade II represents a deep phylogenetic grouping rather than a formal ICTV taxon.

Clade II retroviruses infect a broad range of vertebrate hosts and include numerous medically and veterinary important exogenous viruses. The endogenous record substantially extends the known history and diversity of the clade. In addition to the numerous betaretroviral lineages found in mammalian genomes, ERVs have revealed ancient representatives of the alpha-, delta- and lentiviral radiations, including lineages in host groups from which no closely related exogenous viruses are currently known.

A characteristic feature of Clade II is the use of programmed ribosomal frameshifting in the expression of *pol*. Depending on the retroviral group, one or more frameshift events regulate the production of Gag-Pol or Gag-Pro-Pol polyproteins. This contrasts with the translational readthrough used by many retroviruses in Clades I and III.
