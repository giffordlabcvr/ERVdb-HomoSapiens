Iotaretrovirus
==============

*Iotaretrovirus* is an ERVdb-defined genus of exclusively endogenous retroviruses assigned to *Clade I*. It is not currently recognised as a formal taxon by the ICTV. The genus is represented in ERVdb by the human *ERV-I* and *ERV-ADP*lineages.

No exogenous iotaretrovirus has been identified, and the group remains known principally from primate genomic sequences. Phylogenetic analysis distinguishes these elements from the formally recognised gammaretroviruses and from the other ERV-defined genera within Clade I. The limited number of characterised lineages means that the former host range, diversity and biological properties of the group remain poorly understood.
