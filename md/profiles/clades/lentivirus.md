Lentivirus
==========

*Lentivirus* is a formally recognised genus within the subfamily *Orthoretrovirinae* and is assigned to *Clade II* in ERVdb. The genus includes the human and simian immunodeficiency viruses, feline immunodeficiency virus, equine infectious anaemia virus, bovine and Jembrana disease viruses, and the small-ruminant lentiviruses.

Lentiviruses infect a wide range of mammals, including primates, carnivores and ungulates. They characteristically establish persistent infections and include viruses associated with immunodeficiency, neurological disease, anaemia and chronic inflammatory disorders. In addition to the canonical retroviral genes, lentiviruses possess varying complements of regulatory and accessory genes that influence viral gene expression, replication and interactions with host defences.

Lentiviruses are known predominantly as exogenous viruses, but endogenous lentiviral sequences have been identified in several mammalian groups. ERVdb includes endogenous lineages from rabbits, lemurs, mustelids, colugos and springhares. These genomic fossils demonstrate that lentiviruses have infected mammals over a substantially longer period and across a broader range of hosts than might be inferred from their known exogenous representatives. No endogenous lentiviral lineage has been identified in the human genome.
