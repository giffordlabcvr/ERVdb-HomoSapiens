Rhoretrovirus
=============

*Rhoretrovirus* is an ERVdb-defined genus of exclusively endogenous retroviruses assigned to *Clade I*. It is not currently recognised as a formal taxon by the ICTV. Representative members include the human ERV-Rb, equine EqERV-Rb and rhinoceros Ceratotherium-Rb lineages.

Rhoretroviruses have been identified in primate and perissodactyl genomes, but no exogenous representative is known. The small number of characterised lineages currently limits reconstruction of the group's former host range and evolutionary history. Nevertheless, their phylogenetic relationship supports recognition as a distinct radiation within Clade I.
