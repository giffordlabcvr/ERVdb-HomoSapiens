# Gammaretrovirus

**Gammaretrovirus** is a formally recognised genus within the subfamily **Orthoretrovirinae** and is assigned to **Clade I** in ERVdb. Representative exogenous members include murine and feline leukaemia viruses, gibbon ape leukaemia virus, koala retrovirus and reticuloendotheliosis virus. ERVdb divides the genus into the **BB**, **Eta**, **KoRV–GALV**, **MLV–FeLV**, **RD114**, **REV**, **RfRV** and **T** groups, reflecting major phylogenetic divisions within the gammaretroviral radiation.

Gammaretroviruses infect mammals and birds and include viruses associated with leukaemia, lymphoma, immunodeficiency and other diseases. Their endogenous record is extensive, particularly in mammalian genomes, and includes both ancient lineages and relatively recent germ-line invasions. Koala retrovirus provides a well-studied example of a gammaretrovirus occurring in both exogenous and endogenous forms. Human representatives include the HERV-E, HERV-R and HERV-T lineages, among others.
