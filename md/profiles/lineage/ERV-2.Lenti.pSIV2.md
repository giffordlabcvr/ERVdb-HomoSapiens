# ERV-2.Lenti.PSIV2 (pSIV2)

**ERV-2.Lenti.PSIV2 (pSIV2)** is an endogenous lentivirus lineage identified in Malagasy dwarf lemurs (*Cheirogaleus*spp.). Together with the related pSIV1 lineage in mouse lemurs (*Microcebus* spp.), it provided the first evidence that lentiviruses entered the primate germ line on multiple independent occasions.

Comparative analyses showed that pSIV1 and pSIV2 represent separate endogenisation events that occurred in different lemur lineages approximately 4 million years ago. Their presence in distantly related Malagasy primates indicates that closely related lentiviruses circulated among lemurs during the late Miocene or early Pliocene.
