# ERV-2.Beta.K.HML9 (HERV-K-HML9)

**ERV-2.Beta.K.HML9 (HERV-K-HML9)** is a low copy number ERV lineage belonging to the **K** sublineage of endogenous betaretroviruses (genus *Betaretrovirus*). Comparative analyses indicates that the lineage colonised the genomes of **catarrhine primates (Old World monkeys and apes)** between approximately **17 and 48 million years ago**, with all known human insertions predating the divergence of modern apes.
