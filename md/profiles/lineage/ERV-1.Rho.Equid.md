# ERV-1.Rho.Equid (EqERV.Rb)

**ERV-1.Rho.Equid** (EqERV.Rb) is an ancient clade I endogenous retrovirus lineage related to the **HERV-Rb/PABL** lineage found in the human genome. Orthologous insertions shared by horses, donkeys and rhinoceroses indicate that the lineage was established in the germ line of ancestral perissodactyls during the early Eocene, before the divergence of the major extant perissodactyl lineages, implying an age of at least 54 million years (PMID: 30209175). Most surviving copies are highly degraded, and comparative analyses suggest that the lineage underwent secondary expansion during equid evolution through non-infectious genomic amplification (PMID: 30209175).
