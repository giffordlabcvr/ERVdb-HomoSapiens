# ERV-1.Zeta.W9.9 (HERV-9)

**ERV-1.Zeta.W9.9 (HERV-9)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage that entered the primate germ line during the Eocene or earlier. Although all known proviruses are highly degraded, the lineage generated a large population of descendant long terminal repeats (LTRs) that remain widely distributed throughout the human genome.

HERV-9 is best known as the source of several **LTR12** families, which have undergone extensive amplification during primate evolution. These LTRs contain numerous transcription factor binding sites and have been repeatedly co-opted as promoters and enhancers, contributing to the regulation of nearby host genes.
