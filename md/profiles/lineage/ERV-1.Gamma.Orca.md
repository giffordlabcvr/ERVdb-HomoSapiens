# ERV-1.Gamma.Orca

**ERV-1.Gamma.Orca (KwERV)** is an endogenous gammaretrovirus lineage identified in killer whales (*Orcinus orca*) and other odontocetes, including dolphins, porpoises and sperm whales. It was the first full-length endogenous retrovirus to be characterised in a cetacean.

KwERV retains the characteristic **gag**, **pol** and **env** genes of a simple gammaretrovirus, indicating a relatively recent germline invasion. Multiple loci remain transcriptionally active, although no replication-competent virus has been identified.

Phylogenetically, KwERV groups with the mammalian gammaretrovirus lineage that includes **PERV**, **KoRV** and **GALV**, but its closest known exogenous relative has not been identified. The distribution of related endogenous viruses among odontocetes suggests a complex evolutionary history that may include multiple independent germline invasions rather than inheritance from a single ancestral integration.
