# ERV-2.Lenti.SpELV

**ERV-2.Lenti.SpELV (SpELV)** is an endogenous lentivirus lineage identified in the South African springhare (*Pedetes capensis*). It was the first endogenous lentivirus discovered in a rodent, demonstrating that lentiviruses have historically infected members of the order Rodentia.

Although no complete provirus has been identified, reconstructed SpELV sequences retain characteristic lentiviral features, including a lysine tRNA primer-binding site, a ribosomal frameshift between *pro* and *pol*, and a putative trans-activator response (TAR) element. Molecular dating based on LTR divergence indicates that the lineage circulated in springhares during the Miocene, approximately **8–18 million years ago**.

Phylogenetically, SpELV groups with the endogenous colugo lentivirus **DELV**, forming a distinct lentiviral lineage that has been tentatively termed **Archaeolentivirus**. If this relationship is confirmed, it would indicate that this branch of the lentivirus radiation has persisted for tens of millions of years and extends the known diversity of ancient mammalian lentiviruses.
