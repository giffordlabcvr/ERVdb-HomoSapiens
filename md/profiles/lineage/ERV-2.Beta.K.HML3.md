# ERV-2.Beta.K.HML3 (HERV-K-HML3)

**ERV-2.Beta.K.HML3 (HERV-K-HML3)** is an ancient endogenous betaretrovirus lineage belonging to the **Kappa (K)** sublineage of betaretroviruses (genus *Betaretrovirus*). Approximately 140 proviral loci have been identified in the human genome, making HML3 one of the larger HML lineages. Comparative analyses indicate that the lineage underwent its principal expansion during the **late Eocene**, approximately **36 million years ago**, shortly after the evolutionary divergence of Old World and New World primates.

Unlike **HML2**, which remained active over an extended period and continued generating new proviral variants, HML3 appears to have undergone a relatively brief episode of germline colonisation followed by evolutionary extinction. Phylogenetic analyses recover HML3 as a largely monophyletic lineage containing **two closely related evolutionary subgroups**, while many proviral loci share characteristic deletions within the *gag* or *pol* genes. These shared structural variants suggest that defective proviruses were themselves capable of intracellular amplification before the lineage became inactive (PMID: 12213204).
