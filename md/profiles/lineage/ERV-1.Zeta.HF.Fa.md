# ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fa (HERV-Fa)

**ERV-1.Zeta.HF.Fa (HERV-Fa)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the HERV-F group of primate gammaretroviruses. Together with the related **HERV-Fb** and **HERV-XA** lineages, it forms part of a broader radiation of phenylalanine primer-binding-site (PBS) retroviruses.

Although represented by relatively few loci in the human genome, several proviruses retain recognizable *gag*, *pro*, *pol* and *env* genes. Analysis of paired long terminal repeats (LTRs) suggests that HERV-Fa entered the primate germ line approximately **35-56 million years ago**, consistent with endogenisation during the early evolution of simian primates.
