# ERV-2.Delta.PlaMin

**ERV-2.Delta.PlaMin** is an endogenous deltaretrovirus lineage identified in the Indus River dolphin (*Platanista minor*). It is one of the best-preserved endogenous deltaretroviruses described to date, retaining near-complete *gag*, *pro*, *pol*, *env* and *tax* coding regions.

This lineage (comrpised of a single insertion) is notable for preserving the characteristic regulatory architecture of complex deltaretroviruses. In addition to an intact **tax** homolog, the provirus contains a putative antisense open reading frame between **env** and **tax**, resembling the antisense transcription strategy of modern human and bovine deltaretroviruses.

Phylogenetically, PlaMin groups with the lineage containing **bovine leukemia virus (BLV)**, providing evidence that this branch of the deltaretrovirus radiation has circulated among mammals for millions of years.
