# ERV-1.Iota.ADP (HERV-ADP)

**ERV-1.Iota.ADP (HERV-ADP)** is a clade I endogenous retrovirus lineage belonging to the ancient **Iota** group. The lineage was first identified following the discovery of a retroviral *pol*-like sequence interrupted by the human **ADPRTP1**(*ADP-ribosyltransferase pseudogene 1*) locus on chromosome 13, from which the name **HERV-ADP** was derived (PMID: 7904581).

Although initially characterised from a single defective genomic element, subsequent studies demonstrated that HERV-ADP represents a multicopy endogenous retrovirus family comprising approximately sixty related insertions dispersed throughout the human genome. Phylogenetically, HERV-ADP forms a distinct lineage closely related to HERV-I but is distinguished by characteristic primer-binding site sequences and associated LTR71A and LTR71B families.
