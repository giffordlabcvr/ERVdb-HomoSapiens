# ERV-3.Sigma.Eq (EqERV-S)

**ERV-3.Sigma.Eq** (EqERV-S) is an ancient clade III endogenous retrovirus lineage related to the primate HERV-S group. Orthologous insertions shared by equids and rhinoceroses indicate that the lineage entered the perissodactyl germ line before the divergence of the two major perissodactyl suborders, more than 54 million years ago (PMID: 30209175).
