# ERV-1.Zeta.Equid (Zeta)

**ERV-1.Zeta.Equid (Zeta)** is an ancient endogenous retrovirus lineage found in perissodactyls. Orthologous insertions shared by horses and rhinoceroses indicate that the lineage entered the perissodactyl germ line before the divergence of these groups, more than **54 million years ago**.

Most Zeta loci are highly degraded and no replication-competent representatives are known. The lineage is closely related to the primate **HERV-H/HERV-W** radiation and is notable for including a conserved *env* gene that has been proposed as a candidate equine syncytin. Expression of this gene in placental tissues suggests that, like several independently acquired syncytins in other mammals, it may have been co-opted for a role in reproduction.
