# ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.15 (HERV-15)

**ERV-1.Gamma.Eta.15 (HERV-15)** is a clade I endogenous gammaretrovirus lineage within the Eta subclade. It was originally described as **RRHERV-I** following the discovery of a retinoic acid-responsive endogenous retroviral transcript in human teratocarcinoma cells (PMID: 1920638) and is represented in repeat libraries by the HERV-15 internal consensus and the associated **LTR15** family.

Although first identified through expression of a single defective provirus, the original study showed that RRHERV-I sequences occur as a multicopy family in the human genome. Subsequent phylogenetic analyses recognised RRHERV-I/HERV-15 as a distinct gammaretrovirus lineage, separate from the related HERV-E and HERV-R lineages.
